The Foundation
What Donum Deus means in practice.

Donum — gift. Not a talent, not an inheritance. A gift carries responsibility to the giver. You do not waste a gift. You do not ignore it. You develop it with care.

Deus — the divine source. The body's capacity for strength, adaptation, and recovery is extraordinary. Its tolerance for neglect is finite.

Together, Donum Deus is a standard, not a slogan. Every program built through DONUM is a structured act of stewardship — designed to protect what you were given, develop it with precision, and sustain it for life.

i.
The Gift Is Not Guaranteed
Physical capacity diminishes without intention. Joints degrade. Patterns break down. Strength erodes. Training is not ambition — it is maintenance of something sacred. What you do not protect, you lose.
ii.
Suffering Is Not the Measure
Volume without intelligence destroys the very capacity it intends to build. The measure of a program is not how hard it was. It is how much it returned in strength, quality, and longevity.
iii.
The Long Game Is the Only Game
A body built to perform for decades outlasts and outproduces one built for a season. Longevity is not a constraint on performance. It is the strategy that makes performance possible.
"Do you not know that your body is a temple? Honor it — with every session, every recovery, every choice made in full awareness of what it is."

Methodology
Six pillars.
Zero guesswork.

DONUM does not produce workouts. It produces structured weekly training systems built around your body's actual state, your schedule, and an unbreakable order of priorities. Every program satisfies every constraint before it reaches you.

i.
CNS Management
Maximum two demanding sessions per week. No back-to-back high-output days. Conditioning is never permitted to compromise strength performance. The nervous system is treated as a finite resource — not a target.
ii.
Movement Inventory
Every weekly cycle includes squat, hinge, horizontal push and pull, vertical push and pull, rotation or anti-rotation, gait or carry, and explosive intent. Any gap is corrected before delivery.
iii.
Block Architecture
Warmup, power (when appropriate), strength, accessory, core, mobility. The order is fixed. Volume is calibrated to your current state. Redundancy is removed.
iv.
Fatigue Awareness
Three states: progress, maintain, deload. The program adapts around where you are — not where you wish you were. Volume reduces before intensity. Conditioning drops before accessories.
v.
Schedule Anchoring
High-demand days are placed furthest from your existing obligations and fatigue sources. Lower-body and rotational stress are never stacked. The week works around your life.
vi.
Delivered, Not Generated
Your program is reviewed against every constraint before it reaches you. No partial plans. No default templates. No exercises that do not belong. A complete, coherent training system every time.

Frequently Asked
Direct answers.
How is DONUM different from a generic coaching app?+
Generic apps produce templates. DONUM produces coaching. Every program resolves a fixed priority hierarchy against your actual profile — joint integrity first, performance last. No two clients receive the same output, and every program is reviewed against the methodology before it reaches you.
Do I need a gym?+
No. The intake captures your equipment context — full gym, dumbbells only, or limited setup — and exercise selection adapts accordingly. The movement patterns remain intact regardless of equipment.
What if I am injured or rehabbing something?+
Add the restriction during intake and the program routes around it. For complex, post-surgical, or chronic cases, the Practice or Stewardship tier is the right entry point — a coach's judgment is irreplaceable in those contexts, and you will have direct access to Riz.
How often should I rebuild my program?+
Rebuild when your state changes — schedule shifts, fatigue accumulates, you are returning from a deload, or you have been running the same block for six to eight weeks. Foundation clients can rebuild on demand. Practice and Stewardship clients get in-cycle adjustments as part of their engagement.
What conditioning does DONUM use?+
Short-duration, purpose-driven work: carries, circuits, kettlebell or dumbbell complexes, tempo work, and sled or bodyweight circuits. All conditioning is kept to 10-25 minutes and selected to protect rather than impair the strength work. There is no long-duration steady-state cardio unless it is a stated goal.
Can I cancel?+
Foundation and Practice cancel at any time. Stewardship requires a six-month minimum because the work compounds and meaningful structural change does not happen in shorter windows. After six months, continue month-to-month or step down to a lower tier.
Is this medical or rehabilitation advice?+
No. DONUM is performance coaching. It is not a substitute for a physician, physical therapist, or licensed clinician. If you have an active medical condition, consult your provider before starting.
What happens after I submit the intake?+
Your intake is processed through the methodology, all constraints are resolved, and a complete weekly training program is delivered. You can print it, export it to PDF, or return to rebuild it when your state or schedule changes.
