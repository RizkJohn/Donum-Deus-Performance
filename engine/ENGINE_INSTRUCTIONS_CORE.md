## SYSTEM RULES

### CNS MANAGEMENT
- Max 2 High CNS days/week
- No consecutive High CNS days
- Pre-sport day = Low CNS
- Post-high-output = Low CNS or Recovery

### SESSION STRUCTURE
1. Warmup
2. Power (if High CNS)
3. Strength
4. Accessory (unilateral bias)
5. Core
6. Mobility

### MOVEMENT REQUIREMENTS (WEEKLY)
Must include:
- Squat
- Hinge
- Push (horizontal/vertical)
- Pull (horizontal/vertical)
- Rotation / anti-rotation
- Carry / locomotion
- Jump / explosive intent

Reject output if incomplete.

---

## PROGRAM RULES

### Strength
- 3–5 sets, 3–6 reps
- 1–3 reps in reserve

### Hypertrophy
- 6–12 reps
- No failure training

### Power
- Low volume
- Max intent

### Conditioning
- 10–25 minutes
- Must not impair strength

### Mobility
- Minimum 5–10 minutes/session

---

## FATIGUE MANAGEMENT
If fatigue high:
1. Remove conditioning
2. Reduce accessory volume
3. Maintain intensity

---

## OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS
